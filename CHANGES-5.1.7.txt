SYDALESIS 5.1.7
===============

Mod matrix
- New destinations A/B/C Timbre 5-10. They reach the engine knobs Timbre 1-4 did not cover.
  The matrix menu shows each one by its knob name for the oscillator's current engine:
    Analog     T5 Osc Drift
    Additive   T5 Stretch, T6 Odd/Even, T7 Peak Amount, T8 Comb Shift, T9 Shimmer,
               T10 Partial Detune
    Physical   T5 Stretch, T6 Mode Spread, T7 Coupling, T8 Buzz / Sympathy,
               T9 Exciter Noise, T10 Nonlinearity
    Granular   T5 Scan, T6 Pitch Rnd, T7 Spread, T8 Reverse (sample sources only)
    Spectral   T5 Speed, T6 Stretch, T7 Tilt, T8 Formant, T9 Sparse
  Slots an engine has no knob for are labelled "(unused)" and do nothing.
  Wavetable has no extra targets (Timbre 1-4 already cover its knobs).
- Amount 1 = the knob's full range (Partial Detune 30 ct, Pitch Rnd 1200 ct, Speed 2x).
- These knobs now show modulation rings and accept drag-to-assign / right-drag depth like
  the others.
- They are per voice, so MPE pressure / slide / note random work on them.
- Physical Stretch, Mode Spread and Coupling rebuild the modal bank while they move. CPU
  cost is the same as modulating Decay or Damping already was.
- Grain Shape is not a destination: its window is shared by all voices of the oscillator.

Fixes
- Dropdown menus of the top-bar controls (HQ / quality) opened at the bottom of the window.
  They now open right under the control.

Compatibility
- Presets and sessions from 5.1.6 load and sound unchanged; existing destinations keep
  their numbers.
